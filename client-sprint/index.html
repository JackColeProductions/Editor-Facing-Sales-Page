What the guarantee is

If you buy the VP+ 90-Day Client Acquisition Pass and, after completing every requirement below, you have not booked at least $129 in paid work through VP+ within your 90-day pass, Video Production+ will refund the $129 you paid for the pass in full.

This is a refund of the pass fee. It is not a promise of earnings, and nothing on this page is a projection of what you will earn.

What "$129 in paid work" means

Booking at least $129 means a client, through VP+, commits to and funds work with you whose gross project or retainer value totals $129 or more across your 90-day pass. Gross value is the amount the client agrees to pay, before the VP+ marketplace fee. A single $150 project clears the bar; so does the first month of any retainer at $129 or more; so do several smaller projects that add up to $129.

To be straight about the arithmetic: the guarantee threshold is measured on gross booked value, and the 10% marketplace fee applies to work you book — so booking exactly $129 pays out $116.10 to you. Book $144 or more and your payout itself clears $129.

Your pass window

Your pass runs for 90 consecutive days from the date your VP+ access begins. Day 1 is the day your access starts, not the day you complete your profile.

What you have to do to qualify

  1. Complete an approved VP+ editor profile. Your profile must be submitted and approved, and must include real work samples that are yours.
  2. Submit 100 genuine applications to relevant VP+ jobs inside your 90-day window — roughly 8 a week. An application is genuine when it is sent to a job you could reasonably perform, from your real profile and portfolio, and follows marketplace rules. Duplicate, blank, automated, copy-pasted-to-everything or off-brief applications do not count toward the 100. If fewer than 100 relevant jobs are posted during your pass, applying to every relevant job that is posted counts as meeting this requirement.
  3. Stay reasonably responsive. Reply to clients who contact you about your applications within a reasonable time during the pass.
  4. Have booked less than $129 in paid VP+ work. If clients committed to and funded $129 or more of work with you through VP+ during the pass, the economic outcome has been delivered and the guarantee does not apply.

How to claim

Email hello@videoproductionplus.com within 14 days of your pass ending, from the email address on your VP+ account, with the subject line "Pass Guarantee". We check your application count, bookings and profile status against our records and, where the requirements are met, refund the $129 to your original payment method.

Things worth being straight about

  • The refund is the original $129 pass payment. Not credits, not extra access.
  • One pass guarantee claim per person.
  • Applications and bookings are counted from VP+ platform records. If the records and your own count disagree, the platform records decide.
  • Marketplace fees on work you did book, and anything you earned during the pass, are not affected by a refund.
  • We can decline a claim where the requirements were not met, or where the account breached the VP+ Terms (see section 11).
