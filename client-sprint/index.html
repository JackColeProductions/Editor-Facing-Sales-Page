    Is this a subscription?

    No. The Client Sprint costs $129 once and gives you 90 days of access. There is no automatic monthly renewal.

    What happens after 90 days?

    Your Sprint ends. Nothing is automatically charged. If you want to continue using VP+ afterward, you can choose a membership option separately.

    How does the money-back guarantee work?

    Submit 100 genuine applications to relevant VP+ jobs during your 90-day Sprint. If you complete the guarantee requirements and don't land a paying VP+ client during those 90 days, you can request a full refund of your original $129 Sprint payment.

    What counts as landing a client?

    A client secured through VP+ who commits to a paid project or retainer with you through the platform.

    What counts as an application?

    A genuine application submitted to a VP+ job you could reasonably perform, using your real portfolio and profile, and in accordance with marketplace rules.

    Are there actually new jobs every day?

    VP+ sources new client opportunities continuously, and new jobs are posted throughout the week across different niches, budgets and editing formats.

    What kinds of jobs are posted?

    Both one-off projects and recurring opportunities, from short-form editing to long-form YouTube, ads and other commercial video work. Budgets vary by client and project.

    Is VP+ for beginners?

    The Client Sprint is designed for editors who are ready to take paid client work. You should already be able to edit and have work you can show potential clients.

    What if I land a client before Day 90?

    Great. You keep your Sprint access for the remainder of your 90 days and can continue applying to additional opportunities.

    Does VP+ take a fee when I land work?

    Yes. VP+ takes 10% on what you book through the marketplace. That is the only fee beyond the $129 Sprint payment.

    Can I continue after the Sprint?

    Yes, but only if you choose to. There is no automatic renewal.

    What the guarantee is

    If you buy the VP+ 90-Day Client Sprint and, after completing every requirement below, you have not landed a paying client through VP+ within your 90-day Sprint, Video Production+ will refund the $129 you paid for the Sprint in full.

    This is a refund of the Sprint fee. It is not a promise of earnings, and nothing on this page is a projection of what you will earn.

    Your Sprint window

    Your Sprint runs for 90 consecutive days from the date your VP+ access begins. Day 1 is the day your access starts, not the day you complete your profile.

    What you have to do to qualify

    1. Complete an approved VP+ editor profile. Your profile must be submitted and approved, and must include real work samples that are yours.
    2. Submit 100 genuine applications to relevant VP+ jobs inside your 90-day Sprint window. An application is genuine when it is sent to a job you could reasonably perform, from your real profile and portfolio, and follows marketplace rules. Duplicate, blank, automated, copy-pasted-to-everything or off-brief applications do not count toward the 100.
    3. Stay reasonably responsive. Reply to clients who contact you about your applications within a reasonable time during the Sprint.
    4. Have no paying VP+ client secured during the Sprint. If a client hired you for a paid project or retainer through VP+ at any point in the 90 days, the outcome was delivered and the guarantee does not apply.

    How to claim

    Email hello@videoproductionplus.com within 14 days of your Sprint ending, from the email address on your VP+ account, with the subject line "Sprint Guarantee". We check your application count and profile status against our records and, where the requirements are met, refund the $129 to your original payment method.

    Things worth being straight about

    • The refund is the original $129 Sprint payment. Not credits, not extra access.
    • One Sprint guarantee claim per person.
    • Applications are counted from VP+ platform records. If the records and your own count disagree, the platform records decide.
    • Marketplace booking fees, or anything you earned during the Sprint, are not affected by a refund.
    • We can decline a claim where the requirements were not met, or where the account breached the VP+ Terms (see section 11).
